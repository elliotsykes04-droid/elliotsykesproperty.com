A way out for landlords. A property that stays on your books.

I'm Elliot, a Doncaster-focused property investor. I work with letting agents and their landlords to take on tenanted family homes when the landlord wants to step back, with the tenant staying put and the agent still managing.

For letting agents

When one of your landlords wants out, the usual answer is a sale, and you lose the managed property. I offer another option. I use a delayed completion to take on responsibility for the property, while the tenants stay in place and you keep managing it.

  • You keep the management and your fees. The property stays on your books.
  • The tenants don't move. No notice, no void and no re-letting.
  • Fewer viewings. No open-market sale, so far less disruption for you and the tenants.
  • A speedy exchange. Your landlord gets certainty in weeks, not months.
  • Specialist solicitors handle the paperwork on both sides.

Situations I can help with

These are the landlords I'm best placed to help.

Self-managing and worn out

Landlords who have been managing the property themselves and have had enough. When I take it on, the management can come your way.

Approaching retirement

Landlords who want to step back from tenants and repairs, but have a good tenant they don't want to serve notice on.

Asking for a valuation

Landlords who want to sell, but find that tenants in situ shrink the market and make viewings awkward.

Selling without serving notice

Landlords who want to sell with vacant possession but don't want to serve notice on their tenants. I can agree a deal with the tenants still in place.

What I'm looking for

Tenanted two and three bedroom family homes, mainly from landlords who manage their own properties. I'm focused on Doncaster and the surrounding villages.

Sitting tenants are welcome. I'm happy to keep good tenants in place, so nobody has to move out for a sale to happen.

How it works

  1. A chat

    We talk through the property and what the landlord wants. No obligation, and no cost.

  2. A clear proposal

    I put the terms in writing, usually an agreed purchase with a delayed completion, so I take on responsibility for the property straight away.

  3. Specialist solicitors

    Everything goes through solicitors who specialise in this type of agreement, so everyone is properly protected.
